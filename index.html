About Queue System - Positions & Why Used

These are the Queue - Show Their Positions Using a ?

PositionIndexMeaningOperation
FRONTRES[0]First ordered - Next to cook/deliverGreen - shift()
MIDDLERES[1], RES[2]...Waiting FIFOWhite - waiting
REARRES[last]Last ordered - NewestRed - push()
REAR+1Next indexWhere next order entersBottom - New entry

Example: [Ama FRONT, Kwesi MIDDLE, Yaw REAR] - Top green = FRONT next, Bottom red = REAR last.

1. Fairness FIFO

First ordered gets first. Prevents fights - Ama 10:01 before Kwesi 10:02.

2. Resource Limitation

1 fryer, 1 rider cannot handle 20 at once - needs queue like printer queue.

3. Two Queues for Two Resources

Kitchen Queue cooks in order, Delivery Queue delivers per zone. Without queue food cold.

4. Prevent Starvation & Strict FIFO

Guarantees all Cape Coast towns served. Only push() to REAR, shift() from FRONT.

Flow - Order to Received

  1. Customer enters Name, Phone
  2. Asks Delivery or Face-to-Face?
  3. If Delivery: Choose Town (26 Cape Coast towns) or write custom if OTHER
  4. Auto assigns rider: Abura→0241234567, UCC→0551112223 + Call button
  5. ENQUEUE: PLACE ORDER → Kitchen Queue REAR
  6. DEQUEUE: COOK NEXT → Kitchen FRONT → Delivery REAR
  7. Call Through Rider + Location: Rider calls customer for exact location
  8. I HAVE RECEIVED IT: Receipt with rider number - Let them know received
